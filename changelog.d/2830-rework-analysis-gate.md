### Changed
- **bro debugs the root cause of a task's review FAILs and clears the rework limit itself; you are asked only when the reality drifts from the design** (GH 2830): the new bro-only `task_rework_analysis` tool records a typed analysis of a task's review FAILs as a `task_rework_analysis` audit row: the FAIL ids, finding ids, a `root_cause` (`spec_unclear`, `spec_wrong`, `world_model`, `reviewer_disputed`, `environment`, `swe_execution` or `design_drift`), a `change` (`spec_update`, `world_model_fix`, `effort_raise`, `split`, `retry`, `re_review` or `ask_human`), `approach_shared_cause` (do the findings share one cause in the approach itself?) and a rationale. It refuses a `world_model` cause with any change but `world_model_fix`, `design_drift` with any but `ask_human`, `ask_human` for any other cause, and a shared approach cause with any change but a split or retry. From two FAILs the `rework-limit` gate admits the next swe spawn or resume only when that analysis is newer than the latest FAIL and a row dated after that FAIL proves its change: `task_spec_amended` for `spec_update`, `task_effort_raised` for `effort_raise`, and a world-model scan, reindex, `world_model_concept_upsert` or `Area: World Model` issue for `world_model_fix`. A split, retry, re-review or ask-the-Human analysis never admits one, a read failure denies, and each further FAIL needs a fresh analysis. A `design_drift` analysis stops every swe run of the task, whatever the FAIL count, and its deny tells bro to ask you on the issue with the evidence and options; only you lift it, by typing `waive task <N>`. The fixed caps of six swe runs per task and four unresolved FAILs per issue are gone.
