### Changed
- **bro clears the two-FAIL rework limit itself; the Human is pinged only at the outer ceiling** (GH 2830): the new bro-only `task_rework_analysis` tool records a typed analysis of a task's review FAILs (the FAIL ids, finding ids, a `root_cause`, a `change` and a rationale) as a `task_rework_analysis` audit row. At two FAILs the `rework-limit` gate now admits the next swe spawn or resume once that analysis and its proven change (a `task_spec_amended` or `task_effort_raised` row) are both newer than the latest FAIL; a split, retry or re-review never admits one, and each further FAIL needs a fresh analysis. Six swe runs, four issue FAILs, or two analyses naming the same root cause form an outer ceiling that only the Human's `waive task <N>` lifts, and its deny lists each analysis.
