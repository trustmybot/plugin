### Added
- **task_brief carries the L2 concepts anchored to the task** (GH 2321): the core section adds `concepts {items, truncated}`, up to 5 flows, subsystems and invariants whose steps sit under the task's `files[]` or match a file or symbol in `locations`. Verified concepts come first; suspect and broken ones follow with `flagged: true`, their status and reason, so they never read as fact, and retired ones are left out. Each item carries its matched anchors and its body framed as untrusted content, within 3000 chars, so a swe inside a documented flow reads it instead of re-deriving it from code.
