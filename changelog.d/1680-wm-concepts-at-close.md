### Added
- **swe proposes world-model concepts at close, and the next scan applies reviewed ones** (GH 1680): swe's completion report can now carry up to 5 concepts (`report.concepts`), in the same shape as `world_model_concept_upsert`. The close only checks their shape. Once pr-reviewer passes the task, the next `scan_run` writes each concept as verified and logs a `concept_proposal_applied` audit row. A concept whose anchors are not in the index yet stays pending. If it still fails 14 days after the close, it is dropped with a `concept_proposal_rejected` row that names the error. `trajectory_doctor` adds four warnings: suspect or broken concepts, proposals still pending 7 days after their close, rejected proposals from the last 30 days, and repos with 100 or more indexed symbols but no concepts.
