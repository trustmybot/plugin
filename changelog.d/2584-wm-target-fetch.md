### Added
- **World-model reads pick up merges on the remote within 10 minutes** (GH 2584): the world model is indexed at `origin/<target branch>`, which only moves when something fetches, so a PR merged on GitHub or GitLab stayed out of the index until a later `git` command. Now `world_model_get`, `world_model_search` and `world_model_symbol` first fetch the repo's target branch, at most once every 10 minutes per repo and capped at 5 seconds, then bring the index forward. An offline or slow remote is tried once per window and the read answers from the index it has. Reads by swe and code-reviewer never fetch, since their worktree base is fixed when they start, and a repo with no `origin` remote is skipped. A reindex that follows a fetch which moved the branch is recorded with trigger `fetch` in its `wm_reindex` audit row and in `wm_freshness`.
