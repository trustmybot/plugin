### Added
- **`task_stats` shows how many tokens hook blocks cost** (GH 1915): when a swe run stops, TMB now records its block-turn cost, the context size of the turn right after each hook block (denials and timed-out gates alike), as a lower bound on what the blocks burned. Each spawn carries it as `hook_block_tokens` (null for runs that were never measured), a task's stats carry the total, and `task_stats` with `since` adds `hook_block_tokens`, `hook_block_share` (that total over the runs' `tokens_total`) and `runs_with_cost` (how many runs carry the measure). The trajectory DB moves to schema v59 on the next session start; past runs are not backfilled.
