### Added
- **Review findings the verification gate re-checks on every close** (GH 1737): a pr-reviewer `fail` or `escalate` verdict now has to list its findings, each with a command that shows the problem (`probe_cmd`), what it should show (`expected`) and what it showed (`observed`), and an optional success-criterion id. `validation_record` refuses a `fail` or `escalate` with no findings, and refuses a `pass` that has some. The findings are stored in a new `review_findings` table (schema v47) with ids `F<task>-<n>`. On every close attempt the verification gate runs each finding's probe after `verification[]`: a passing probe marks an open finding fixed, a failing probe blocks the close, and a fixed finding whose probe fails again is called out as a regression. Probe runs are recorded with the verification run as `[probe <id>] <command>`, and `task_brief` lists the open findings under `open_findings`.
