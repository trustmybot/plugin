### Changed
- **FLOWS.md records the quality-layers design** (GH 2866): a new Quality layers section sets the two axes every flow is built on, responsibility and method, and the rule that no two layers overlap: each check has one owner. Six layers hand off in order, each with a typed contract: the Human owns intent, bro readies the environment deterministically, bro writes the spec by judgment, swe owns functionality and passes every deterministic check before it closes, code-reviewer owns the diff without re-running swe's checks, and bro judges whether the change fits the design. Principles: gated handoffs, leaks point upstream (the leak rate per layer is the health metric), CI confirms and does not discover, and failures return to their owner. RESPONSIBILITIES.md links to it. The flow tables are unchanged; GH 2866 task C aligns them.
