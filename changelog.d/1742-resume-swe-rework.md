### Added
- **Resumed swe reworks are gated, chained and measured** (GH 1742): when bro resumes the swe that wrote a commit with `SendMessage`, a new `swe-resume-gate` hook judges the message like a fresh swe spawn of its task. It denies at the rework limit with the same reason the spawn gate gives, and it denies when the swe ran at an effort other than the task's current one, pointing to a fresh spawn at that effort. Messages to other agents, or to a swe of a closed task, pass. The resumed swe's next `task_brief` opens a run row of its own, linked to its previous row by the new `agent_runs.resumed_from` column (schema v49). That row meters only the transcript entries after it started. `task_stats` adds `reworks.fresh` and `reworks.resumed`, each with count, wall-clock time, tool calls and cache-read tokens.
