### Changed
- **code-reviewer judges swe's checks from their records and backs every pass with its own probes** (GH 2866): the reviewer reads each recorded check's exit code, time and output tail and runs none of them again; a record that does not prove its criterion is a FAIL with a probe. A pass records at least one probe of what the diff's dependents rely on, each run in a review worktree. swe checks that the callers of each changed symbol still compile, are called right and pass their tests; the reviewer probes whether the changed behaviour breaks what those dependents rely on, and does not redo swe's caller list.
