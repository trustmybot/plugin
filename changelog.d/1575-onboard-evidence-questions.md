### Changed
- **`/onboard` shows what it found and recommends from it, in plain words** (GH 1575): each question now opens with the scan's facts about your repos (for example "6 merges into dev, 1 into main") and why one option is recommended; that option comes first and is pre-selected when the evidence is clear, so a repo that merges into `dev` defaults to Git Flow and `dev`, and a folder whose repos have no remote defaults to Local-only. Branching and PR target are asked once when every repo points the same way, and repo by repo otherwise. Every option is reworded to say what happens to you rather than how TMB works inside, and a new L1 lint (`tests/l1-lint/onboard-option-wording.sh`) keeps internal terms out of option labels and descriptions.
