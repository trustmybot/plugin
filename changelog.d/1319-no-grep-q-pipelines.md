### Fixed
- **No more SIGPIPE flakes from `| grep -q` under pipefail** (GH 1319): `grep -q` closes the pipe on its first match, so the upstream command could die of SIGPIPE and a `set -o pipefail` pipeline failed although the pattern was there — the L6 pre-flight smoke check `l5_smoke_mcp` in `tests/l5-l6/lib/smoke-helpers.sh` aborted on the large `tools/list` response, and L1 lints flaked at close gates. Every such site under `tests/`, `scripts/` and `hooks/` now pipes into `grep PAT >/dev/null`, which reads to EOF, and the new L1 lint `tests/l1-lint/no-grep-q-in-pipelines.sh` fails on any `| grep -q` in a pipefail script.
