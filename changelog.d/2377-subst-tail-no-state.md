### Fixed
- **The write-path guard no longer follows a cd or an assignment that is only an argument after a substitution** (GH 2377): in `echo $(true) cd /w/ok; touch x` or `echo $(true) D=/w/ok; touch $D/x`, the words after `$(…)` or a backtick are arguments of echo, yet the guard changed its dir or bound the variable and judged `/w/ok/x` instead of the path the shell writes. Those words now change no dir, `$OLDPWD` or variable, and their redirects still count. A real cd or assignment (after `;`, `&&`, `||`, `|`, a newline, or as its own statement's command or prefix, as in `x=$(echo a) cd /w/ok`) is still tracked.
