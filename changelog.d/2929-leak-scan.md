### Added
- **swe's close blocks a commit that adds a secret, an email address or a home path** (GH 2929): beside the changelog and stale-doc checks, the close gate scans the lines the branch adds since its merge base with the PR target. It looks for secrets with secretlint's recommended rules, for email addresses, and for paths such as `/Users/<name>/`, `/home/<name>/` and `C:\Users\<name>\`. A hit blocks the close with each hit's file, line and rule, and never shows the matched text. Deleted lines and binary files are not scanned. `noreply@` addresses, addresses at `example.com`, `example.org` and `example.net`, and placeholder home paths such as `/Users/<name>/` or `/home/runner/` pass. To admit an intended hit or turn a rule off, commit `.config/tmb/leak-scan.json` (`{"version":1,"allow":[{"rule":"email","pattern":"<regex>","reason":"<why>"}],"disable_rules":[{"rule":"home_path","reason":"<why>"}]}`); every entry needs a reason, and an invalid file blocks the close. Inline `secretlint-disable` comments are not honored.
