### Added
- **The dashboard Issues page can filter by one label and show stats for it** (GH 2509): a Label menu next to search lists every label by issue count, and picking one keeps only the issues that carry that exact label (stored as `label=` in the page address, so the view can be shared or bookmarked). A collapsible Label stats panel then appears above the list with an open-vs-closed donut, the other labels those issues carry (top 8 plus Other, each with its open and closed counts), and time to close in hours: median, mean (flagged as skewed by outliers) and the closed count, with median and mean shown as — until at least 3 are closed. Each card can switch to a table or download CSV. Clear the label from the menu or its filter chip to get the full list back.
