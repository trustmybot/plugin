### Fixed
- **A reviewer identity observation is tied to its call and expires, and an honor-system verdict can no longer downgrade a verified one** (GH 1303): the `reviewer-identity-observe` hook now records the call's `tool_use_id` and `observed_at` on the `reviewer_identity_observed` row, and `validation_record` consumes only the newest unconsumed row observed within the last 600 s — an older row left by a failed or denied reviewer call is never consumed, so it cannot stamp a later caller (the call fails `subagent_session_unavailable` naming the row's age and the reviewer re-calls). An `mcp_available: false` call refuses to overwrite a verified (`mcp_available = 1`) verdict with `verified_verdict_exists`; a verified call may still replace a verified row. `trajectory_doctor` gains the warn contract `no_unconsumed_reviewer_observations` (unconsumed rows older than 1 h), and the schema test no longer writes rows on the reserved issue -1.
