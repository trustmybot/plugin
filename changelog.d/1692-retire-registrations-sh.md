### Removed
- **`scripts/hooks/lib/registrations.sh` and the world model's dispatch-chain parse are gone** (GH 1692): the awk helpers that read `dispatch.sh` chains and router rows for shell scripts are deleted, and the symbol index no longer reads that file. Hook symbols of the shipped `hooks/hooks.json` are unchanged, since router entries take their gates from `dist/hooks-router.txt`. **Limit:** scanning an old checkout whose `hooks.json` still registers `dispatch.sh` chains now gives each chain entry one `runs` edge to `src/hooks/<chain>.ts` instead of one per chain member.
