### Changed
- **Task status is an append-only event log at schema v77** (GH 3224): every status change is a `task_events` row checked against an edge chart, and the database refuses a direct status write, a move with no edge, a wrong from-state or a move missing its record. The states are now pending, running, submitted, failed, closed, superseded and cancelled: `completed` and `needs_validation` became `submitted`, and `escalated` split into `superseded` (retried, with `retry_of` on the new task) and `failed`. Swe closes with `task_update_status(status='submitted')`; `task_retry` supersedes any task not already superseded or cancelled, with no escalate step first; an issue close cascade and a dropped feature branch cancel; a bro move by hand needs a `note`. The upgrade backfills one event per existing task.
