### Fixed
- **Concept claim args are documented, and a wrong shape fails by name** (GH 1840): `world_model_concept_upsert` and `task_update_status`'s `report.concepts` now share one claim-args schema that lists every field and the claims that use it: `exists` `{path, qualified_name}`, `calls` `{from: {path, qualified_name}, to: {path, qualified_name}}`, `only_writers_of` `{table, writers: [path]}`, `tested_by` `{path, qualified_name, test_path}`. A claim whose args have the wrong shape now fails naming the field and the shape it expects (`fail: from must be {path, qualified_name}`) instead of the misleading `fail: no such caller`. Well-formed claims evaluate as before.
