### Fixed
- **A reviewed task rebased by an older `cli.js integrate` keeps its review** (GH 2546): a `task_integrated` row written before GH 2082 has `reviewed_sha` but no `review_anchor_at`, so it anchored at its own time, after the code-reviewer pass, and `issue_close` and `bro_atomic_close` refused with `tasks_without_review`. Such a row from `hook:integrate` now takes the review anchor of its `reviewed_sha`, walking back through chained rows of the task (at most 10 hops) to the earliest `bro_verification_pass` or carried anchor. A row from any other author, a reviewed SHA with no anchor, a newer fail verdict, or a pass older than the anchor still needs a fresh review.
