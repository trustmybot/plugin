### Fixed
- **A suite run no longer holds the shared suite slot forever** (GH 3249): `test-run.js` now runs its command in a process group of its own under a hard budget, from `--budget-s <n>`, else `TMB_TEST_RUN_BUDGET_S`, else 1800 s. When the budget runs out it stops the group (SIGTERM, then SIGKILL after a grace), frees the slot, prints `budget Ns exceeded; killed process group P` and exits 124. SIGTERM, SIGINT or SIGHUP to `test-run.js`, or its parent going away, also stop the group and free the slot. The swe-verification-gate stops a running command's group and frees its slot when it gets SIGTERM, SIGINT or SIGHUP itself, so a killed close hook leaves no orphaned suite behind. The three benches under `mcp/trajectory-server/src/test/` now exit at once when a bare `node --test` collects them; one such sweep had held the slot for 30 minutes.
