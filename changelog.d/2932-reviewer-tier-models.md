### Added
- **Reviewer risk tiers can pick the reviewer's model, not only its effort** (GH 2932): a new `config_set` key, `reviewer_tier_settings`, gives each risk tier (`prompt_bearing`, `high_effort_paths`, `docs_tests`, `default`) an optional `model` (`opus`, `sonnet`, `haiku`, `fable`) and `effort`. For example, `{"docs_tests": {"model": "sonnet", "effort": "high"}}` has a docs- or tests-only change reviewed by Sonnet at high effort instead of Opus at low. The setting applies whenever `reviewer_effort_tiers` (or the tiered arm of `reviewer_effort_ab`) picks the tier. `review_handoff` then records the model in the `reviewer_effort_assigned` row and returns it in `reviewer_spawn`. The code-reviewer spawn gate admits only the assigned model, and only a spawn with no model when none is assigned. A tier with no entry, or with no `model`, keeps the code-reviewer's own model, and the fixed `reviewer_effort` never sets one. With the key unset or malformed, reviews are assigned exactly as before.
