### Fixed
- **The merge-driver L3 test no longer fails after all its assertions pass** (GH 1361): `tests/l3-integration/hooks/merge-driver.test.sh` removed its scratch directory with a bare `rm -rf` in its EXIT trap, and a late git write under the fixture repo's `.git` could make that fail with "Directory not empty". The fixture repo now runs with `gc.auto=0`, `maintenance.auto=false` and `GIT_OPTIONAL_LOCKS=0`, and the new `tests/lib/tmpdir-cleanup.sh` helper waits for child processes and retries the removal up to three times, failing the test only when the directory is still there after the last attempt.
