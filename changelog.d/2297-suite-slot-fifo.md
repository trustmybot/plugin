### Fixed
- **swe closes wait their turn for a suite slot instead of failing with `suite slot busy`** (GH 2297): the verification gate waited only 3 s for a suite slot at the 1800 s `verification_timeout_s`, and waiters raced for a freed slot in no order, so a swe's `task_update_status(status='completed')` was denied again and again. The trajectory-server PreToolUse hook timeout is now 3630 s, so the gate waits up to 1800 s at that budget. Waiters for a slot, from the gate and from `test-run.js`, now queue first come first served: each writes a ticket under the lock root's `queue/` and refreshes it every poll, and a ticket left unrefreshed for 10 s by a crashed waiter is dropped. A waiter that can't write its ticket still gets a slot by polling as before, with one stderr line.
