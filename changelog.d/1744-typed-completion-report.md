### Added
- **Typed swe completion reports and a capped reviewer spawn note** (GH 1744): each top-level `## Success Criteria` bullet now starts with an id (`- SC1: ...`), and `task_provision`, `task_update_spec` and `task_create_batch` refuse a spec without them. On a move to `completed`, `task_update_status` takes a `report` with one `met` / `not_met` / `not_applicable` entry per id, plus optional extras and fixed findings. `task_brief` returns the report, and `bro_atomic_close` refuses a close the report does not cover. Bro passes reviewer context as a `spawn_note` of up to 300 characters on `bro_atomic_close` or `task_reopen`, and a new spawn gate denies a pr-reviewer prompt carrying more prose than that beyond its anchors (bypass: `TMB_SKIP_PR_REVIEWER_SPAWN_NOTE=1`).
