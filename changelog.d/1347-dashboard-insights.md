### Added
- **An Insights tab in the dashboard charts what your agents spend and how well they do** (GH 1347): five sections for tokens, cost, time, quality and throughput, each filterable by period, repo, agent, model and effort, with the view kept in the URL. Tokens compares swe and reviewer runs on one scale and bro sessions on their own, with p50 and p95 marked once there are 20 runs. Cost stays off, with a note saying so, until model prices are set. Every chart can switch to a table or download as CSV, and works from the keyboard. The tab loads on demand, so the rest of the dashboard opens as fast as before.
