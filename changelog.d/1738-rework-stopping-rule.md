### Added
- **A stopping rule for rework** (GH 1738): after two review FAILs since a task's last pass, a third swe spawn for it is denied until something changes the plan. That is a `kind='decision'` discussion newer than the latest FAIL (on the task or its issue), or any comment from the Human. The deny lists the ways forward: write a decision naming the change of approach, `task_retry` with a redesigned spec, split the task, or ask the Human. Bro is also told, once per task, when a `running` task passes 60 minutes (`TMB_TASK_OVERRUN_MIN` changes the limit), with the minutes elapsed, the swe spawn count and the current blocker. `task_stats` now reports `wall_clock_ms`, `review_fail_count`, `swe_spawn_count` and `rework_count`.
