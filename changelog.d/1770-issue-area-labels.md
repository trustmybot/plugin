### Added
- **Optional area label on every issue** (GH 1770): a project can now list its areas in `issue_area_labels`. Once set, `issue_create`, `issue_update` (when labels are passed), `intent_start` and Codex `planning_issue_create` require exactly one area label, alongside the classification and priority labels. A missing area is refused with `missing_required_labels` and two or more with `multiple_area_labels`; both errors list the valid areas. You can also map path prefixes to areas in `issue_area_paths`. When no area is passed, the paths named in the objective and description pick it (the longest prefix wins), the area is added to the labels and the result reports it as `area_inferred`. Paths that fall in two or more areas are refused with `ambiguous_area`, which names the candidates. Projects that leave both keys unset see no change.
