### Fixed
- **The dist-fresh lint no longer reports false drift in `dist/hooks.js` depending on where the build ran** (GH 1850): the hooks bundle skipped the postprocessor that the other two bundles go through, so its esbuild module keys kept a `../../…/node_modules/.bun/` prefix whose depth depends on the checkout location, and a worktree build differed from the main checkout's. The build and the lint now postprocess `dist/hooks.js` and its map too, and `bundle-whitespace-safety.sh` fails when any bundle in `build:bundle` skips the postprocessor or when a committed bundle still carries such a key.
