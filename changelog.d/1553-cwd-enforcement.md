### Added
- **A repo-wide lint keeps the cwd out of location, and every hook deny is audited** (GH 1553): `tests/l1-lint/no-cwd-reads.sh` replaces the hooks-only `no-cwd-identity.sh`. It reads `scripts/`, `skills/*/scripts/`, the trajectory server's `src/` and `tests/lib/`, and fails on a `$PWD`, `pwd` or payload `.cwd` read, a `git` with no `-C`, a `process.cwd()`, or a `spawn` / `execFile` with no `cwd`. Each exemption lives in `tests/l1-lint/cwd-allowlist.tsv` with a category and a reason, and an entry with no reason or matching nothing fails. `dispatch.sh` now writes one `hook_denied` audit row for every deny it returns (`hook`, `rule`, `tool`, `agent_type`, `task_id`, `cwd`) on the task's issue, else `-1`, so a deny that never reaches bro still leaves a trace; a failed write never changes the decision.
