### Fixed
- **`cheatcode_vet` can inspect plugins from the official Anthropic marketplace that live inside the marketplace repo** (GH 2135): `cheatcode_search` passed a marketplace-relative `source` such as `./plugins/pr-review-toolkit` through as-is, so vet could not read it and answered `caution` with the "could not be inspected" rationale and no capabilities. Search now gives these plugins a github.com folder URL in the marketplace repo (`https://github.com/anthropics/claude-plugins-official/tree/main/plugins/pr-review-toolkit`), which vet lists and whose manifest it reads. Absolute URL and `{url}` sources are unchanged; a source that climbs out of the marketplace (a `..` segment or a leading `/`) is not resolved and falls back to the plugin's `homepage`.
