### Changed
- **Four more read tools return concise output by default** (GH 1958): `audit_list`, `validation_history`, `discussion_list` and `task_get` now take `response_format: 'concise' | 'detailed'`, defaulting to `concise`. Concise `audit_list` leaves out `content_json`; concise `validation_history` leaves out `feedback`, `probes` and `verification_run` and cuts each finding to its id, criterion, status and severity; concise `discussion_list` cuts the body of every row that is not a decision or intent to 200 characters and marks it `body_truncated: true`; concise `task_get` leaves out `completion_report`, which `task_brief` already returns. Pass `response_format: 'detailed'` for the full rows. A `fields` projection returns the columns it names, as before, and an unknown `response_format` value is refused with the valid values.
Metric: tokens_per_task lower
