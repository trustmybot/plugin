### Changed
- **code-reviewer reviews a task before bro closes it** (GH 2866): the handoff order is now swe → code-reviewer → bro. A new `review_handoff` composite hands a completed task's commit to code-reviewer without changing its status: it refuses without a completion report covering every `SC<n>` id or without a passing verification run for the commit, records the review anchor with the swe's out-of-scope paths and their reasons, assigns the reviewer effort, stores the spawn note and returns the ready-made `reviewer_spawn`. `bro_atomic_close` now runs after the verdict: it refuses with `review_pass_missing` until a pass follows the handoff of the task's current commit, keeps the scope gate and its waiver, and no longer re-checks the report or the run. The code-reviewer spawn gate admits a completed task handed off at its commit, or a closed task, and its deny names `review_handoff`. `task_reopen` accepts a completed task, so a FAIL is reworked without a close, and the worktree is removed when `bro_atomic_close` closes the task after the pass. Tasks closed under the old order keep their review anchors, so they still push.
