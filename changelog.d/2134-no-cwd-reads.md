### Fixed
- **Merge, attribution and verification hooks act on the right repo** (GH 2134): the attribution footer and the auto-close on merge called `gh` / `glab` in the hook's own directory, so a `cd <repo> && gh pr create` or `gh pr merge` from another directory read, edited or closed the same number in a different repo, or did nothing.
