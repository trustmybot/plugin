### Changed
- **`task_provision` plans on a current world model** (GH 2958): before it resolves the base, it fetches `origin/<target branch>`, ignoring the 10-minute fetch window but keeping the 5 s cap, and brings the world model to that commit. When the fetch fails or runs past the cap, the task is still provisioned on the current index and a new `wm_provision_fetch_failed` audit row records the repo, branch and error.
- **The `wm-read-before-provision` hook now denies** (GH 2958): `task_provision` goes through only after a world-model read in the last 60 minutes that covers one of the task's `files[]`, either by naming the path, or a directory above or below it, in the read's input or its result. Otherwise the call is denied with the `world_model_get` call to make, and the `wm_read_missing_before_provision` row records why (`no_read`, `no_covering_read` or `transcript_unreadable`). A repo with no world model yet is still allowed, with the old nudge.
