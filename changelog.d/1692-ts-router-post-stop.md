### Changed
- **PostToolUse, PermissionDenied, Stop and SubagentStop now run their gates through the TypeScript router** (GH 1692). `hooks/hooks.json` has one exec-form entry per group: PostToolUse `mcp__.*trajectory-server__.*`, `Bash` and `Agent`, PermissionDenied `Agent`, Stop and SubagentStop. Only SubagentStop keeps `onFailure: block`, for `consultant-persistence-gate`.
  - Gates in a group now run one at a time in a fixed order, where the separate `dispatch.sh` entries ran in parallel. On PostToolUse `Bash`, `attribution-footer` (16 s budget), `clean-merged-branch` (11 s) and `close-issue-on-merge` (23.3 s) now share a 23.3 s group budget: a slow merge's reaction holds the call longer than it did, and a gate left no time is skipped and logged. On PostToolUse trajectory-server, `cleanup-worktree-on-task-close` (11.8 s) runs before the rest under a 15.7 s group budget.
  - A block outside PreToolUse prints `{decision: block, reason}` and keeps the other gates' `additionalContext` in the one output. An unparseable payload blocks only on SubagentStop and PreToolUse; on the other events it logs to stderr and exits 0.
  - `budgets.json` group keys are now `<event>:<matcher>`, and an empty matcher is `-` in `dist/hooks-router.txt`. `TMB_HOOKS_IMPL=bash` and the headless shim keep working on the new events.
