### Changed
- **Dashboard tables look like GitHub's** (GH 1790): the Tasks, Runs & gates and Audit tabs, and every other table in the dashboard, now use GitHub's DataTable style: a search-icon filter, a muted header with sort arrows, hover rows and an empty-state message when nothing matches. Tasks show the title with its task and issue number, a status label, the branch and an effort counter. On a phone, secondary columns hide so the page no longer scrolls sideways. Every timestamp now reads relatively ("3 hours ago", "yesterday") with the full date on hover, and a time just under a day reads "23 hours ago" instead of rounding up to "24 hours ago".
