### Fixed
- **Startup catch-up scans are audited as `startup_catchup`, and `world_model_symbol` source reads handle two edge cases** (GH 1851): the scan the trajectory-server runs when it connects was recorded as a post-close scan (`bro_auto_post_close`). The `symbols_md5` docs now describe the stamp as `content_md5 || ':' || EXTRACTOR_VERSION`.
