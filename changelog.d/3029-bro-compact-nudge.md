### Added
- **bro suggests a /compact once a session's context gets large** (GH 3029): on a bro turn, when the context of bro's latest call (input, cache-creation and cache-read tokens) is above the new `bro_compact_nudge_tokens` config key, bro gets one advisory line asking it to have you run /compact at the next natural break; the compaction restore brings back the issue, its tasks and the newest decision. It fires at most once per session, recorded as a `bro_compact_nudged` audit row, and never blocks. The default is 80000 tokens; change it with `config_set` (a non-negative integer) or set it to 0 to turn the nudge off.
