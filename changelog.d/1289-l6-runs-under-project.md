### Changed
- **L6 chain runs live under the project and run with an isolated HOME** (GH 1289): `tests/l5-l6/run-l6-chain.sh` wrote its runs to `~/.claude/tmb/l6-chain-runs`, the last documented TMB write outside the project. Runs now land in `<project>/.claude/<plugin>/l6-chain-runs/<run-id>/` (`<project>` = `CLAUDE_PROJECT_DIR`, else the repo root; mode 0700; `L6C_RUNS_DIR` still overrides; gitignored), and L5's preserved trajectories in `<project>/.claude/<plugin>/l5-trajectories/`. The chain runs with `HOME=<run-id>/home`, reaching the model cache, mise and the OAuth token through env, runs its pre-flight `claude -p` calls under `<run-id>/preflight-home`, and fails at exit, listing the entries, if that HOME holds anything but `.cache/huggingface`. A new `--self-test` flag checks the path resolution, the HOME isolation and the exit assertion with a stub step runner and no model calls. `release-gate.yml` uploads the L6 artifact from `${{ github.workspace }}/.claude/tmb/l6-chain-runs/`, and `no-home-writes.sh` now scans the L5/L6 runners and their libs. Runs from earlier versions stay under `~/.claude/tmb/l6-chain-runs` until removed (UPGRADE.md).
