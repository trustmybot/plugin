### Fixed
- **A SWE that ends its turn to wait on its own background command no longer has its task auto-completed** (GH 2206): `swe-atomic-close` completed any `pending` or `running` task with commits on SubagentStop, even when the SWE had only paused until a background command it started finished. The hook now reads the SWE's transcript, and while a background command it started has neither a completion notification nor a `TaskStop`, the task keeps its status, one `swe_atomic_close_background_wait` audit row names the command ids, and bro is told the SWE's resumed turn closes the task. A fresh checkpoint handback still takes precedence, and an unreadable transcript keeps the old behaviour.
