### Fixed
- **swe runs keep one identified row from spawn to close** (GH 1502, GH 1500): the Agent spawn now moves a pending task to `running` and opens its swe `agent_runs` row with `spawn_id`, `session_id` and `effort`; the swe's `task_brief` binds that row to its `agent_id`, so a live spawn is no longer swept as an orphan after 10 minutes, and a resumed spawn reuses its row. A committing swe stop now completes a `running` task, and the latest SubagentStop sets `exit_reason`, so a task that closed after a resume no longer shows its swe run as `failed`.
