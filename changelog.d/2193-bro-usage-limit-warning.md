### Changed
- **Bro is told when a long session has become expensive** (GH 2193, GH 2239): once the mean input context of the session's last 5 main-thread calls passes 120000, each bro turn carries a one-line warning naming that mean, the limit and the session's first call as the cold-start reference, and asks bro to finish what is in flight, end the session and resume cold from the DB. The measure reads the recent calls in the session transcript, so the warning clears once compaction shrinks the context. It is a nudge only: nothing is blocked, and sessions under the limit see no change.
