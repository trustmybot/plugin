### Changed
- **A code-reviewer pass now has to show the checks it ran, and the brief carries each check's output** (GH 2866): `validation_record` refuses a `pass` without a non-empty `probes[]` of `{target, probe_cmd, observed}` (`probes_required`), or with a malformed one (`probes_invalid`), and writes nothing. Fail and escalate may carry probes but don't need them. The probes are stored in the new `validation_attempts.probes_json` column (schema v68; older rows stay NULL), and `validation_history` returns them as `probes`. The honor-system `validation-record-fallback` CLI applies the same rule to a new 7th argument. `task_brief`'s `verification_run.commands[]` now carry `output_tail`: the last 1000 characters by default, or the whole stored tail with `response_format: 'detailed'`. A reviewer can read why a check failed without running it again.
