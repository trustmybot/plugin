### Fixed
- **A shell write to a project skill or agent hidden in a huge brace expansion now needs the Human's approval** (GH 2994): a word whose braces expand past what can be listed was judged as if every middle word stayed under its leading dir, so `git checkout -- z/{1..5000}/../../{a,.claude}{,x}` could restore the `.claude` dir with no approval. Such a word with a `..` segment, a root, or a `$` or backtick now counts as a write to the project's `.claude` dir, and the deny names that dir to approve. Tee and redirect targets get the same reading, and a `..` after a brace now resolves per expanded word, so `echo x > {.claude/skills/a,b}/../q` is gated too. Overflowing words with none of these, and commands that write nothing, are allowed as before.
