### Changed
- **The prompt-author lint flags only bare negations** (GH 2863): the two classes that flagged every `Don't`, `Never` and `do not` line are now one `bare-negation` class. A prohibition that names what to do instead (`instead`, `rather than`, `unless`, `only when`, `only if`, `except`, a ` — `, a `;` or `, but`) or marks a hard limit (all-caps `NEVER` or `MUST NOT`, or the `LOAD-BEARING-SAFETY` marker) is no longer flagged, matching the prompt-engineering guide's rule to keep a prohibition for a hard limit and pair it with an alternative. The lint stays advisory and always exits 0.
