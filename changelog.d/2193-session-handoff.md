### Added
- **A bro session past its usage limits hands over to a fresh one** (GH 2193): when a session stops past its limits with no subagent, background task or cron still running, a new Stop hook records its issue and last message and shows one notice asking you to start a new session. The next startup or `/clear` in that folder within 24 hours picks the handoff up in its opening context, with the issue's live tasks read fresh, and uses that issue as the pending one. The hook never blocks or continues a turn. Sessions sharing a folder can take each other's handoff. Set `TMB_DISABLE_SESSION_HANDOFF_HOOK=1` to turn it off.
