### Fixed
- **A chain gate's watchdog never kills an unrelated process** (GH 1326): the watchdog that stops a trajectory-gates member past its budget sent KILL to the gate's process group without checking that the chain was still running it, so after the dispatcher was killed it could signal a process group id the system had since reused. It now kills only while its chain subshell is still its parent and the gate still leads its own group. The dispatcher header and `scripts/hooks/README.md` also say that a TERM sent to the dispatcher's pid alone lets the running gate finish, while TERM to its process group stops both.
