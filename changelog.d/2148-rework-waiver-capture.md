### Fixed
- **Only you can waive a task's rework limit** (GH 2148): an agent could record a "Human" decision itself through `discussion_append(author='human', verified_human=true)`, so a bro-written row waived the rework limit and one task ran 6 swe runs and 4 failed reviews. Now `discussion_append` refuses `author='human'` from every caller and the `verified_human` argument is gone. Typing `waive task <N>` (or `Waive Task #N`) in your own prompt is the one way to write a Human row: a new UserPromptSubmit hook, `rework-waiver-capture`, records it as a decision on task N, with the line you typed as its text. Any other wording, a task that does not exist, or text from a subagent or notification records nothing, and the hook never blocks your prompt.
