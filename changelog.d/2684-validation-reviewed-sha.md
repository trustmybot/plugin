### Fixed
- **Each validation attempt now records the commit its review covered** (GH 2684): when every spawn gate allows a code-reviewer spawn whose prompt carries `task_id=`, `attempt_n=` and `commit_sha=`, a new `record-reviewer-spawn` step writes a `reviewer_spawn_observed` audit row. `validation_record` copies that row's commit into the new `validation_attempts.reviewed_sha` column (schema v65), and `validation_history` returns it, including through `fields`. Bro can now check a passing review against a PR head from the DB, even after a merge moves the task's own `commit_sha`. A denied spawn writes nothing, a failed write never blocks the spawn, and attempts with no observed spawn keep `reviewed_sha` NULL.
