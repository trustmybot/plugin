### Fixed
- **A reviewer retry no longer leaves an orphaned identity observation** (GH 1316): when `validation_record` consumes a `reviewer_identity_observed` row, the same transaction marks every older unconsumed row for that `(task_id, attempt_n)` with `superseded: 1`, `superseded_by` (the consumed row's audit id) and `superseded_at`; a superseded row is never consumed, a stale-row refusal or an honor-system call without a fresh row supersedes nothing, and `trajectory_doctor`'s `no_unconsumed_reviewer_observations` skips superseded rows, so a call that failed on bad args and was retried no longer warns after an hour while a truly orphaned row still does. A new L3 case shows a main-loop `validation_record(agent='pr-reviewer')` is denied by `agent-arg-matches-caller` and leaves the reviewer's row unconsumed.
