### Added
- **Every merged change gets before/after statistics on the agent metrics** (GH 2600): the new read-only `change_impact` tool takes a PR number and compares the runs on builds that include it with the runs on builds that do not, within 14 days of the merge and at most 200 runs a side. It covers swe rework ratio, tokens per task, reworks per task, review fail rate, first verification pass rate, hook-block tokens per swe run and bro tokens per session. For each it reports both sides' count, median and mean, the ratio of medians with a 95% confidence interval, a Mann-Whitney p-value corrected across the metrics, and a verdict: improved, regressed, no change, or not enough data under 20 runs a side. Runs from before builds carried a version are placed by time and counted as estimated, and `measured_with` names the other PRs that shipped in the same builds, so their effects cannot be told apart. Without a PR number it lists the last 20 merged PRs. The same numbers come from `node --experimental-sqlite dist/change-impact/cli.js --db <path> --change <N>`.
